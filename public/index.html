▶ THE RULES

  1. Pick 4 Premier League players, any position, any club.
  2. Aim for an aggregate of 21 league goals. Cup goals and own goals count for nothing.
  3. You are only eligible to win if all 4 of your players score at least once.
  4. The moment you go over 21, you are eliminated.
  5. 100% of the pot goes to whoever finishes on 21, or the next closest score below it — with 4 different goalscorers.
  6. If several finish level on the best score, the pot is split equally.
  7. No transfers, no changes. Injuries and moves are part of the game.
  8. Data is sourced from the official Fantasy Premier League statistics — Premier League goals only, own goals excluded — and settles any contentious goal.
